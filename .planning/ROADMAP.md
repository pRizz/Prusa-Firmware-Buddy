# Roadmap: Prusa Firmware Buddy Rust Port

## Overview

The v1.0 milestone established the Rust+Bazel rewrite evidence foundation: source-backed baseline, Bazel authority, typed Rust contracts, subsystem parity manifests, aggregate verification, and clean archival metadata. Future milestones continue phase numbering and should turn the remaining non-local proof categories into durable CI, simulator, hardware, release, and maintainer-review gates.

## Milestones

- ✅ **v1.0 Rust Port Evidence Foundation** — Phases 1-12, 38 plans, shipped 2026-06-15. Archives: [roadmap](milestones/v1.0-ROADMAP.md), [requirements](milestones/v1.0-REQUIREMENTS.md), [audit](milestones/v1.0-MILESTONE-AUDIT.md).
- 📋 **v1.1 Cutover Evidence Hardening** — requirements and roadmap not yet defined. Expected direction: turn non-local simulator, hardware, live-service, release-candidate, signing, retained-code acceptance, and maintainer-review blockers into executable and reviewable gates.
